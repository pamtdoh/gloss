# Touch keeps the fixed bottom bar — iOS owns the callout

Nothing changed on coarse pointers, deliberately. iOS Safari collapses
the selection on any tap and floating popovers near it are unwinnable
(the round-8 research), so the captured selection still persists as a
painted highlight and its actions still live in a stable bottom bar
with the quoted text. The pointer split restores the desktop
experience without reopening the iOS fight.
