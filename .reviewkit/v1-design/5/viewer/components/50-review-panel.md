# The review panel: quick comments up top, one card per note

Quick Comment offers three one-tap presets (Not needed / Simplify /
Defer, keys 1–3) that write ordinary whole-fact comments — undoable,
editable, deletable like anything else. Below it, Notes lists one card
per item: comments with their quote and Markdown-rendered text,
questions with threads and Reply, a kebab menu for edit/delete, and
drifted/detached anchor states with a re-anchor action. Hovering or
focusing a card highlights its quote in the fact; the panel collapses
to a badge rail on desktop and lives in a bottom sheet on mobile.
