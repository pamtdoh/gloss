# Ink & Amber is ReviewKit's own token layer

Warm near-monochrome ink neutrals with a single burnt-amber accent and
squarer corners — chosen by the owner from three candidate directions,
replacing the palette borrowed from leetcoach. The architecture stays:
light and dark defined once as tokens (dark follows the system), an
elevation scale per theme, 20ms paint-only hovers with exits on the
sharper curve, and one composable focus ring on every control. Reduced
motion zeroes everything.
