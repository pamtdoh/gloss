# Ink & Moss is ReviewKit's own token layer

Warm near-monochrome ink neutrals with a single muted Everforest-green
accent — the owner picked it from terminal-scheme-inspired candidates
after finding the first amber too loud; amber survives only as the
changed/stale semantic. The architecture stays: light and dark defined
once as tokens (dark follows the system), an elevation scale per theme,
20ms paint-only hovers with exits on the sharper curve, and one
composable focus ring on every control. Reduced motion zeroes
everything.
