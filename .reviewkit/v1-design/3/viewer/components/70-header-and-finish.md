# The header carries progress, attention, and the one terminal action

Content-keyed seen tracking feeds a tabular-numeral progress readout
that survives snapshot iteration; a question pill announces "N answered
— your turn" via a polite live region and jumps on click; snapshots
switch through a radix Select. Finish opens a summary sheet listing
every decision and item before confirming; Approve sits behind an
explicit confirm dialog because promotion to `approved/` is the one
irreversible act.
